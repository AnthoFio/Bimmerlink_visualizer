# Session persistence and export

## Description

Remember how the user configured the view, and let them export charts and share view configurations.

## Outcome

coming back to a similar log restores the working view; results can leave the app as images or JSON.

## Done when

- coming back to a similar log restores the working view; results can leave the app as images or JSON.
- All 4 stories of this epic are done.

## Boundaries

- Out of scope for the whole initiative: backend and accounts, live OBD/ENET streaming, writing or editing logs, multi-log comparison.
- Belongs to sibling epics: Foundation and AIDD workflow; CSV upload and parsing; Parameter catalog enrichment; Dynamic chart rendering; Parameter selection and grouping; Chart exploration interactions; Axis and scale control; UX quality: performance, accessibility, theming, privacy.
